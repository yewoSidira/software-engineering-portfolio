Featured Project

CampusConnect

A university engagement platform designed to centralize announcements, events, clubs, and communication while demonstrating scalable backend engineering practices.

Problem

Universities often rely on fragmented communication channels such as WhatsApp groups, notice boards, social media pages, and email. This creates missed announcements and inconsistent information flow.

Solution

CampusConnect provides a centralized platform where administrators, student organizations, and clubs can publish verified announcements, manage events, and communicate effectively with students.

Architecture

The system follows a modular backend architecture focused on scalability, security, and maintainability.
